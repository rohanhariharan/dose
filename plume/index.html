This is the map view. The forecast layer will take today's field plus the wind field and predict where tomorrow's air goes, trained on historical frames for one region.

Method & limits

The field is sampled on a 5° global grid (2,520 points) from the Open-Meteo air quality API, which serves CAMS model output — not ground-station measurements. Values are hourly model estimates, so treat this as a plume-shape view, not a compliance reading.

Wind arrows show the 10 m flow direction, reversed from meteorological "from" direction. That is the mechanism the forecast model has to learn: pollution leaving one grid cell has to arrive in the next one, shifted by the wind vector.

Arrows are drawn on a 10° subsample for legibility. Hover the map for the interpolated value at any point.
